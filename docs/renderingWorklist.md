# Rendering fidelity worklist

What the preview does not yet draw as the client (RoF2) does, ordered by what it blocks. How the client renders what is done is in [clientRendering.md](clientRendering.md); `calibrateShot` measures the remaining difference against claudelink shots of Claude's spare RoF2 client, and `getToolingStatus` lists every shot's latest run.

Live-only content (models, looks, and textures the RoF2 client lacks) is not rendered: placement lists it and moves on. A difference that remains for RoF2-era content whose data matches the Peridot server's database is a renderer bug.

## First: what exported zones need

Blender-built zones drawn as the client would draw them depend on these.

1. **EQG zones.** `importZone` reads client EQG zones by the loader rules in [clientRendering.md](clientRendering.md#eqg-zones) (the loose version 2 `.zon` over the archive's version 1, baked light only where its count fits, skinned models posed by their `_DEFAULT` animation), and `importZoneFile` reads exported ones. Still to draw: the colors the client makes for some placements whose baked light doesn't fit (the Guild Lobby's), how the `SModel` draw lights an object without baked light (its vertices carry `0xFF808080` where the file stores no color; the preview gives it the full share of scene light and no baked light), the `SModel` effect families' other terms, and what the `MPL` materials (most EQG geometry) still do otherwise: point light 0 through the normal map, an `MPLGB` material's glow, and `MPLBasic`'s fixed-function stages and `MPLBlend`'s two diffuses (their coverage maps and vertex colors draw; clientRendering.md, EQG zones). Not settled in game: what a short `.lit` gives past its colors, and the skinned models' 3.125 drop. The preview also lights a `createMaterial` normal map with the scene light, where the client's region effect (terrain) uses a normal map only for point light 0; the object effects are not traced.
2. **Point lights and emitters remainder.** Both draw ([clientRendering.md](clientRendering.md#point-lights), [Particle emitters](clientRendering.md#particle-emitters)); what each section lists as not settled remains: the region of influence and the split of EQG terrain into draws (the preview chooses lights per vertex), the particles' near clip plane, the draw order between emitters, and whether blended zone surfaces hide particles behind them. Previews draw the viewer's carried light (`carriedLight`; `calibrateShot` where the dump puts it, which differs from the trace's offset, clientRendering.md, Point lights); other characters' carried lights are not drawn, nor a light held at a model's attachment point.
3. **Sky remainder.** The dome, sun and moon, and horizon band draw from the client's sky files, and a zone with a sky takes its light and fog color from it ([clientRendering.md](clientRendering.md#sky)). Still to draw: clouds, stars, the backdrop ring, and the dome's and band's colors by azimuth. The moon lights the scene before 5:55 and after 18:20, as traced; whether it lights a classic zone is not settled (clientRendering.md, Point lights), and the shots that settle it are named there. Not traced: the satellites' turn and fade, whether the sky draws before or after the world, and why the client lights no moon after its clock jumps from midday to night (clientRendering.md, Calibration), and the moon's direction (21:00 shots fit the light taken an hour on better than at the clock, noon ones the clock).
4. **Texture orientation in the DLL.** Static EQG models need v flipped and skinned ones do not; this is measured from screenshots, not traced. Find the vertex buffer step that differs (static effects read texture coordinates as shorts over 256, skinned ones as floats) so export writes what the client expects.
5. **Additive blending.** `EQGraphicsDX9.dll` has one additive pass (source and destination factor one, fog off, `0x10088ac5`), chosen through a descriptor per shader type (`0x1009a3ec`). Which shader types take it, and so whether `AddAlpha` does, is not traced; `AddAlpha` draws opaque.

## Next: matching client zones in calibration

6. **Load-time light of placed objects.** A classic zone's placed static objects without colors of their own draw with the colors the client computes once the zone has loaded ([clientRendering.md](clientRendering.md#placements-and-their-vertex-light)), matching ten zones' RoF2 dumps in 99% of first colors; for 70 of 6548 the drop finds a different floor than the client's, for a reason not found. EQG objects whose baked light doesn't fit seem to get the same light (the Guild Lobby's, alpha `0x19`) and still draw with none.
7. **Character lighting.** Spawns are lit like zone meshes; the client lights them with the `SkinMesh*` effects, not yet read.
8. **Equipment.** NPCs and players draw without equipment (Luclin bodies show bare skin); Drakkin armor pieces and layers are not drawn either. claudelink's dumps carry each spawn's equipment and armor colors.
9. **Levels of detail.** The client switches `.lod` models by distance (for example `OBJ_pinetree` 100, 200, 500, 5000 units); every object draws at full detail.
10. **Radial flora.** EQ terrain zones draw grass cards around the camera (`radialfloradefs.rfd`, the ecosystems' flora layers); the import counts them in `radialFloraNotDrawn`.
11. **Liquids.** EQG water, waterfall, and lava materials draw by their effects ([clientRendering.md](clientRendering.md#eqg-zones)), at the effect time a view sets, with these stand-ins: opaque water where the client's alpha is fresnel times 2.9 and more; see-through liquids (falls, waterfall ribbons) layering among themselves in Blender's order of their objects' origins, which for water bodies is the world's, so two overlapping in a view may layer wrongly; no point light 0 term on water or lava, so lava's crust takes point light 0 through the vertex light with falloff where the client lights it through the normal maps without falloff, and an object's lava glows at once its second diffuse where point light 0 brightens it up to twice. Not settled: that an empty environment sampler reads black (an RoF2 screenshot of Argath's or Sepulcher's pools, 2D `coverage_cool`, at a grazing angle, and `Logs\dbg.txt` after entering showing `CreateCUBETexture(): unable to load`); the cube faces' orientation, D3D's standard table being assumed and borne out by one streak in Highpass Hold (an RoF2 screenshot of Crescent Reach's Bear Cave pond from (-150, -2030) facing east at eye level, midday, against the preview's from the same camera); whether water without `e_TextureDiffuse0` draws (1,600 triangles, Buried Sea's isles' lakes and Brell's Rest's `regwater`; the import leaves them out; an RoF2 screenshot of Blacksail Folly's lake); what water whose diffuse no archive ships draws (Cooling Chamber's `obj_mobjecta` and `obj_mobjectc_pan`, 20 triangles naming `rc_cavewater_c.dds`, drawn black as a missing texture and listed in `missingTextures`); lava in game at all (no RoF2 screenshot of lava exists; Brell's Rest or Underquarry at eye level). The import counts the water and lava triangles drawn with these stand-ins in `drawnOtherwise` (`waterOpaque`, `lavaPointLightByVertex`). EQ terrain water (the tiles' levels and sheet rectangles, `water.dat`'s sheets) is not drawn; the import names it in `waterNotDrawn` ([clientRendering.md](clientRendering.md#not-drawn-yet)).
12. **EQ terrain remainder.** The tiles' point lights (named in `lightsNotDrawn`); the ground merged by the land error (the preview draws every quad, not the client's simplified mesh); layering maps and child layers (no zone ships either; refused); a layer whose first cover map does not load and tiles without ecosystems (neither in this client; refused); the colors the client reads past the end of a `.lit` shorter than its count (drawn without baked light, listed); how group members without baked light are lit (as objects without baked light).
13. **Scene light sources.** The special ambient of a view's character, its share of scene light, in EQG and EQ terrain zones (drawn in classic zones) and the second special ambient source (scene graph vtable `0xd0`); previews draw a character without infravision or ultravision, whose ambient floors differ (clientRendering.md, Where the lighting values come from), and `calibrateShot` refuses a viewer with either.
14. **Cover map mips.** D3DX recompresses each generated mip level of a terrain cover map to DXT5; the preview box filters without recompressing.
15. **Spawn looks not read.** Appearance values some EQG models carry (an ALA's face style and heritage) are refused rather than guessed.
16. **WLD normals.** The client reads each WLD normal byte through a table in steps of 1/15 ([clientRendering.md](clientRendering.md#zone-files-as-the-renderer-reads-them)); the preview draws them divided by 127 (the load-time light reads them through the table).

## Validation

Rules the renderer uses that are not yet confirmed against the RoF2 client itself. Code traces read what the DLL does but can miss a step. Each of these needs RoF2 evidence: claudelink shots of the spare RoF2 client, the client's `Logs\dbg.txt` (the graphics DLL logs there, including every baked light it ignores), or a read of the running client. claudelink's `screenshot` takes a zone dump in the same frame as the shot: the zone header, clock, and sky, the graphics camera (position, orientation, view angle, clip planes, projection scales), the viewer's carried light and share of scene light, the graphics settings, every spawn, door, ground item, and placed object, every actor in the scene (definition, position, orientation, scale, and a simple actor's vertex count and baked light colors), and the engine's property tree. A shot with its dump settles most of the rules below.

- **Texture orientation** of static EQG models (unchecked against the RoF2 client).
- **EQ terrain:** 32 texels per tile (the DLL's default distance table; `eqgame.exe` may set others); a sampler without a texture drawing black (Crypt of Shade, [clientRendering.md](clientRendering.md#eq-terrain-zones)); tilt order; the cover and blend maps' mip filtering.
- **Objects without baked light:** drawn with no baked light and the full share of scene light, an assumption. Classic objects take the lit batches path on the CPU while `LitBatches: Enabled` is set, as in the owner's client (`0x10055810`, `0x1009d670`), EQG objects the `SModel` effects; EQG objects whose baked light doesn't fit keep none in Freeport West and get colors the client makes in the Guild Lobby (clientRendering.md, EQG zones). A classic zone's placed static objects get colors from their placement or at load (worklist 6).
- **Tilt order** of EQG zone placements.
- **Models drawn without colors.** A WLD object mesh without vertex colors gets `0xFFFFFFFF` in the client's object builder (`0x10057060`), which `SModelC1` would draw at full texture brightness; a zone's placed objects get colors at load instead (worklist 6), but a door, ground object, or model placed by hand that never gets any is drawn here with no baked light and the full share of scene light. A RoF2 night screenshot of a door whose model stores no colors (with its MQPeridotEmu dump) would show which.
- **Back faces.** The preview draws every face from its front only, a placed model's liquid among them, and a liquid on the zone's terrain from both sides ([clientRendering.md](clientRendering.md#back-faces)), settled for zone and object faces by Freeport West's passage shots (`shot_20261012-000042-930152`, `shot_20261012-000030-964124`), for placed models' liquids by canyonhousing's one-sided fall, not drawn from behind (`shot_20261016-003108-956326`, `shot_20261016-003046-676228`), and for terrain liquids by Crescent Reach's terrain falls (`shot_20261011-234802-737437`, 7.3 drawn from both sides against 9.0 culled), a whole view from 400 up. Confirmed for placed models' liquids by canyonhousing's falls rebuilt with a back (`editWater` with nothing changed) and exported in two parts: drawn from the front, from inside the hall, and side-on (`shot_20261019-160822-387161`, `-160834-247650`, `-160844-351532`). Planned calibration, to confirm terrain liquids: a Crescent Reach terrain fall seen from behind (from above its lip, looking down along its back), drawn there. Alpha-tested cards are culled too, settled by East Freeport's single-faced window grille (`shot_20261013-035140-291105`, `shot_20261013-035148-612667`); classic WLD cards nearly all carry an offset back face of their own, so no classic card in a reachable spot is single-faced. Unshot: blended surfaces other than liquids, culled by the same rule as the client's art suggests (most carry a back face of their own); a spare shot from behind an untwinned one settles it, as would tracing `D3DRS_CULLMODE` in `EQGraphicsDX9.dll`'s draw setup (the effects set none).

## Parked

- **Other particle effects** (spell effects, actor emitters from `ActorEmittersNew.edd`, WLD particle clouds): not drawn until the owner asks; a zone's environment emitters draw. Placements count WLD particle clouds in `particleCloudsNotDrawn`.
